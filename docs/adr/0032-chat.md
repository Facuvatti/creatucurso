# ADR-0032: Tutor-student chat (implementation of ADR-0010)

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-22

## Context
ADR-0010 chose an in-app chat on Postgres. This records how Phase 9 was built and the rules decided on the way.

## Decision
1. **Tables** in schema `chat`: `conversation` (course, student, staff; unique per triple), `message` (text and optional attachment) and `read_position` (`last_read_at` per person and conversation; unread = messages from the other person after it).
2. **Who can talk.** One rule, a single SQL (`PAIRS` in `src/areas/chat/chat.ts`) used by start, list, open, send, mark-read and attachment download, always with the tenant of the request Host: a student with an `active` enrollment in a course, and a staff person of that course (a tutor assigned to it, or an owner/admin of the academy as the creator). Students cannot message students; a tutor only reaches students of assigned courses. If the enrollment stops being active (refund, revoke) or the tutor is unassigned, the conversation disappears from lists and is refused; the rows stay and come back if access is restored.
3. **Privacy.** Messages are visible only to the two participants. Owners and admins have no screen or endpoint to read other people's conversations (they take part only as the staff side of their own). Database administrators can still read the table.
4. **Real-time.** `GET /api/chat/stream` is a Server-Sent Events route per signed-in person and Host. Sending a message emits on a plain in-process `EventEmitter` (kept on `globalThis`), to both participants. Heartbeat comment every 25 s, `retry: 3000`, listeners removed on disconnect, the page reconnects itself and reloads the list and open thread after a drop. Caddy needs `flush_interval -1` on `/api/chat/stream`. This only works with ONE web process; with several, the emitter must be replaced by Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY. Not built now.
5. **Attachments.** PNG, JPEG or PDF, 5 MB (`CHAT_ATTACHMENT_MAX_BYTES`). The type comes from the magic bytes; images are re-encoded with ffmpeg without metadata and at most 2000 px wide; a PDF is stored as sent and is only served as a download. Files live in `MEDIA_ROOT/chat/<conversationId>/<messageId>.<ext>`, outside what nginx serves, and leave only through `GET /api/chat/attachment/<messageId>` after the participant check (nosniff, sandbox CSP, no cache). Uploads go through `POST /api/chat/send/<conversationId>` (multipart with a custom header against cross-site forms). Text is stored raw and escaped by React when rendered. Limit: 20 messages per minute per person.
6. **Retention and export.** Messages are kept as long as the conversation exists and are deleted by cascade with the course, the academy or the user. Attachment files are not deleted by the cascade (a later cleanup job can remove `chat/<conversationId>` folders without a row). There is no automatic purge and no export in v1.

## Consequences
- Not exercised yet: this release was written without running Node; the owner's acceptance run must cover SSE through Caddy, uploads and the unread badges.
- Email notification of unread messages is left to the automations mechanism (ADR-0026) later.
